Shorts Farm Uploader

Shorts Farm Uploader is a private, internal tool used by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads videos created by the owner to YouTube channels that the owner manages.
  • Sets basic video metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish time).
  • Lists the owner's own channels and uploaded videos to manage them.

Who can use it

The tool is not a public service. It is used only by its owner with the owner's own Google accounts. No third-party users sign in to it, and it does not access anyone else's YouTube data.

Google and YouTube data

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload and manage the owner's own videos. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
